It's been a little over ten years since I started this blog, so I figured it
was probably about time to give it a bit of attention.

The biggest change is under the hood: I migrated from Jekyll to
[Hugo](https://gohugo.io/), and from GitHub Pages to Cloudflare. The site
already feels noticeably faster, which is nice. Apparently generating a
mostly-static website doesn't need to take very long after all.

I also got rid of Disqus. Let's be honest, who's still using Disqus in this
decade?

The rest was deliberately kept fairly simple. I wanted something that still
feels like the same blog, just cleaner, easier to navigate, and, perhaps most
importantly, actually nice to use on a phone. After ten years of accumulated
CSS and various bits of ancient web archaeology, that alone felt like progress.
Yay 2020s.

There are a couple of new sections too: **[Projects](/projects/)**, where
I've collected some of the things I've built or contributed to over the years,
and **[Talks](/talks/)**, because apparently I've been giving talks for long
enough that they deserve their own page.

And yes, there's finally **dark mode**. You can now turn the light off. It was
about time.

The banner above is AI-generated. I'll admit it. But I think it looks pretty
damn slick, actually based on a real image and it seemed somewhat appropriate.

That's pretty much it. No grand relaunch, no new business venture, no
blockchain involved.

Just a slightly less terrible website. And if you spot one of my many tpyos,
I'd be more than happy to accept your correction (via GitHub).

Welcome back.
